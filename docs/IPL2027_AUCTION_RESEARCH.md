Status: **our reading from public reports and our own IPL ball-by-ball (Sportmonks, IPL 2013-2026; figures below are 2024-2026 unless marked).** The official register with base prices is due **4 Dec 2026**; franchises shortlist by 1 Dec; the auction is **16 Dec 2026 in Goa (ITC Grand Goa)**. Price calls are our opinion, to be labelled "our model" on screen. Players with no IPL minutes cannot be judged from our data.

## 1. How hard is it to get a slot?
- 66 slots against 1,220 names is **1 in 18.5** before shortlisting. Overseas: 25 slots for 281 (1 in 11). Indian: 41 slots for 939 (1 in 23). The 925 uncapped Indians compete for part of those 41: **about 1 in 22 or worse**.
- Last cycle the IPL cut 1,355 registrations to 350 (26%) for 77 slots (reports, Dec 2025). If the same share holds, expect **about 300-330 names on the 4 Dec list, roughly 5 per slot** (our projection, not reported).
- Franchises can add names to the register until 27 Nov (CricTracker) and submit final shortlists by 1 Dec: nothing below is a confirmed final-list place.

## 4. Who could go big (our opinion; demand = teams with the gap, supply = proven players)
1. **Trent Boult**: both phases; four bidders with real gaps (LSG, DC, CSK, KKR hold ₹156 cr). Age and 2026 availability are the discount. Top-three price.
2. **Matheesha Pathirana**: the same four buyers for the best proven death bowler. May cost as much as Boult.
3. **Deepak Chahar**: the Indian powerplay bowler with no overseas-slot constraint; any of LSG, DC, KKR, CSK, PBKS can bid. His death economy is the risk.
4. **Wanindu Hasaranga**: scarcity: MI and SRH (₹72.75 cr between them) both need spin and he is the only proven wrist-spinner.
5. **Jake Fraser-McGurk**: the opener scarcity (DC, KKR, SRH, ₹121 cr). Our data shows an elite powerplay striker; the risk is consistency.
6. **Avesh Khan** (Indian, ₹2 cr base): LSG, DC, PBKS need pace; his death record is better than his price suggests.
7. **Jonny Bairstow**: all-phase batting, bids from the batting-gap sides.
8. **Gerald Coetzee / Spencer Johnson / Anrich Nortje**: pace demand spills over; Coetzee is the most economical of the three.
9. **Shimron Hetmyer**: a finisher premium, with weak death numbers.
**Watch for the surprise:** Theekshana at ₹1 cr and Karun Nair at ₹75 lakh offer the most strength per rupee; either could draw bids well above base from MI/SRH and KKR/DC.
**Not in the market on the reports:** Travis Head, Mitchell Starc, Ajinkya Rahane (retired). Priyansh Arya, Mayank Yadav, Rajat Patidar, Digvesh Rathi and Rahul Chahar are retained by their teams.
