Status: **leads and our reading, not a confirmed field.** The IPL has not published the registered list or base prices (reported: registration closes 23 Nov, franchise shortlists ~1 Dec, final list ~3 Dec, auction 16 Dec 2026 at 14:30 IST, Goa, Star Sports and JioHotstar). Registering does not guarantee a place on the list or a contract. Several auction-tool and "players list" sites carry made-up or mislabelled content (some describe a finished Abu Dhabi auction); they were ignored.
Numbers below marked "our data" are from our Sportmonks ball-by-ball (IPL 2025-26). Needs come from `docs/IPL2027_NEEDS.md`. Price calls are our opinion and must be labelled "our model" on screen.

## 2. Released names that fit, with our numbers (IPL 2025-26)
**Pace**
- **Trent Boult** (MI release): 73 overs, economy 9.2, 25 wickets; death economy 9.1 over 119 balls. The only released bowler who is good in the powerplay and the death. Named by cricketnews for LSG, KKR and DC. Reported only 5 matches in 2026 (Cricinfo).
- **Matheesha Pathirana** (KKR release): 43 overs, economy 9.1, 14 wickets; death economy 9.7 over 143 balls, the most death balls in the pool. Named for LSG, KKR, DC and CSK.
- **Mitchell Starc** (DC release): 58 overs, economy 9.5, 30 wickets, death economy 9.4. Cricinfo's live blog headline says "Starc unavailable for IPL 2027": treat as not in the market until the list shows him.
- **Deepak Chahar** (MI release): 66 overs, economy 9.2, 20 wickets, but death economy 12.0: a powerplay specialist, Indian (no overseas slot needed).
- Others: Lockie Ferguson (PBKS) 22 overs, economy 10.3; Dushmantha Chameera (DC) 17 overs, 10.6; Spencer Johnson (CSK), Tushar Deshpande (RR), Akash Deep (KKR), Avesh Khan (LSG, powerplay economy 12.7); Adam Milne, Kwena Maphaka, Gerald Coetzee have little or no data in our archive.
- **Mustafizur Rahman** (KKR release) and six more Bangladeshis have registered (India.com, 12 Nov, via the BCB): Nahid Rana, Hasan Mahmud, Taskin Ahmed, Shoriful Islam, Rishad Hossain (leg-spinner), Towhid Hridoy (batter).
**Spin:** the pool is thin. Wanindu Hasaranga (LSG release): 41 overs, economy 8.9, 11 wickets, the only established wrist-spinner. MI and SRH need spin most. Others released: AM Ghazanfar (MI), Mayank Markande (MI), Keshav Maharaj (MI), Rehan Ahmed (DC), Swapnil Singh (RCB), George Linde (LSG): little or no recent data, so unproven.
**Openers and batters**
- **Pathum Nissanka** (DC): 10 innings, 278 runs, strike rate 159. **Abhishek Porel** (DC): 16 innings, 401 runs, SR 142. **Nitish Rana** (DC): 20 innings, 442 runs, SR 156 (an Indian middle-order option). **Karun Nair** (DC): 9 innings, 216 runs, SR 164. **Nehal Wadhera** (PBKS): 21 innings, 434 runs, SR 140.
- Travis Head (SRH) is the best opener in the pool by results (167 powerplay strike rate over 358 balls) but Cricinfo reports he has opted out of 2027; Ajinkya Rahane is recorded as retired (Wikipedia).
**Finishers:** the thinnest market. Shimron Hetmyer (RR): 317 runs in 20 innings, SR 136, death-over SR only 139 (the league's is ~171). Shahrukh Khan (GT): death SR 180 over 51 balls. Mitchell Owen (PBKS), Mahipal Lomror (MI), Matt Short (CSK) have too little data.
**All-rounders:** Prashant Veer (CSK release): 91 runs in 5 innings, SR 136; no bowling data; bought for ₹14.2 cr last auction, the most expensive release (Cricinfo). Rachin Ravindra (KKR release) and Jacob Bethell (RCB release, recovering from knee surgery per Cricinfo).

## 4. Who could command the biggest bids (our opinion, ranked by demand against supply)
Demand = purse of the teams with a matching gap and an overseas slot where needed; supply = how many pool players have a proven record in that role. No base prices exist yet, so no price predictions: last cycle's reference points are Prashant Veer ₹14.2 cr (an uncapped Indian) and Travis Head ₹14 cr, both reported by Cricinfo.
1. **Trent Boult**: proven in both phases; wanted by LSG (₹47.45 cr, 3 overseas slots), KKR (₹41.65 cr, 3), DC (₹39.5 cr, 4), CSK (₹28.05 cr, 2). Four buyers with 156 cr of purse between them. The strongest case for the top price, tempered by age and his 5-match 2026.
2. **Matheesha Pathirana**: the best proven death bowler available, young, and the same four buyers. A bidding war is likely even if he costs less than Boult.
3. **Wanindu Hasaranga**: the only established wrist-spinner, with MI (₹32.6 cr, 2 overseas) and SRH (₹40.15 cr, 3) both needing spin, and DC, PBKS and CSK partly. Scarcity pushes the price more than his numbers (economy 8.9).
4. **Deepak Chahar**: an Indian powerplay bowler with no overseas-slot constraint; any of LSG, DC, KKR, CSK, PBKS can bid. The risk is his death overs (economy 12.0).
5. **Pathum Nissanka**: the best realistic opener, wanted by DC, KKR and SRH (121 cr of purse). Head and Rahane are not in the market.
6. **Nitish Rana**: an Indian middle-order batter with 442 runs at 156; named for LSG and KKR; Indian-slot friendly.
7. **Shimron Hetmyer**: scarce finisher, but his death-over strike rate (139) is below the league's. Price likely driven by scarcity, not results.
8. **Prashant Veer**: his ₹14.2 cr price tag last year will anchor talk; CSK are named as the buy-back.
9. **Mitchell Starc / Travis Head**: would top any list if they enter, but both are reported unavailable. Re-check on 3 Dec.
10. **An uncapped riser from SMAT** (Ankit Kumar, Shivam Shukla, Tushar Raheja): uncapped bids can jump well past their base on the day; the three fit needs at DC/KKR/SRH (opener), MI/SRH (spin) and KKR (keeper).
Constraints that cap prices: GT (₹8.75 cr) and RCB (₹14.15 cr) cannot bid for the stars; league-wide there are only 23 overseas slots and ₹298 cr.
