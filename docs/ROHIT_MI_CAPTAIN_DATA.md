- **Career:** 7,331 IPL runs and 324 sixes in 276 innings, strike rate 132.9 (Cricsheet 2008-2026). Sportmonks and Cricsheet agree season by season except 2013 (537 vs 538) and 2020 (332 vs 334): quote official figures, or avoid those two seasons on screen.

## 5. Angles the data supports (with the line we must not cross)
1. **"The record he is coming back to lead":** five titles, five finals, playoffs in 6 of 10 full seasons, against MI's 17-27 under Hardik. Say what happened; do not say Hardik's captaincy caused it.
2. **Myth or Data: "Did MI collapse without Rohit?"** True that results fell (55.4% to 38.6%, 6 of 10 playoff seasons to 1 of 3). Not provable that the captain is why: the squad, injuries and the auction cycle also changed. A fair verdict would be "half busted".
3. **Myth or Data: "Rohit bats freer without the captaincy":** the strike rate jumped from 130 to 151, but the league's powerplay rate rose by a similar amount, and Rohit stayed level with it in both eras. Verdict: busted.
4. **Season-by-season chart of MI wins with captain bands** (2008-2026) and the five title years marked: the cleanest visual.
