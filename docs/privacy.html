Privacy Policy

The Third Man Publisher · Last updated 12 November 2026

What this app is

The Third Man Publisher is a private tool used only by the operator of The Third Man cricket channel to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public, and nobody other than the channel's operator signs in to it.

What it accesses

When the channel's operator signs in with Google, the app requests only these permissions:

  • Google Drive (drive.file): to upload the channel's rendered videos, subtitles and images to Drive, and to move earlier versions of those same files into an archive folder. This permission only covers files the app itself created. The app cannot see or read any other files in Drive.
  • YouTube (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl): to upload the channel's videos, and to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions.

What it stores

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data. It does not use analytics, cookies or advertising. Sign-in tokens are held only temporarily while a publishing task runs. They are never written to the code repository or shared with anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
